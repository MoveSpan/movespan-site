Efficient breathing supports the brain and body

When ventilation exceeds the body’s current metabolic demand, carbon dioxide may fall. Carbon dioxide helps regulate cerebral blood flow and supports oxygen release from hemoglobin into tissues.

Brain circulation CO₂ helps regulate blood-vessel diameter in the brain.
Oxygen delivery CO₂ participates in releasing oxygen into active tissues.
Calm regulation Quieter breathing may reduce unnecessary respiratory effort.
Breathing rate and movement pattern alone do not diagnose hyperventilation or low CO₂. MoveSpan uses them as wellness observations to personalize practices.
