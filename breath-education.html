Your Breath Snapshot

These measurements describe three different parts of your current resting state. They are most useful when viewed together and repeated under similar conditions.

Breathing rate describes tempo

Your breathing rate shows how many complete breathing cycles occur in one minute. It describes breathing frequency, but does not show where movement occurs or how much effort is used.

Breathing pattern describes movement

Your breathing pattern describes where you noticed the clearest movement: higher in the chest, through the lower chest, around the abdomen and lower ribs, or mainly in the lower abdomen.

Resting pulse describes heart rate

Resting pulse shows how many times the heart beats per minute during this measurement. It can vary with recent activity, heat, hydration, caffeine, sleep, illness, medication, emotional state, and individual fitness.

A single Breath Snapshot is an observation, not a diagnosis. Repeated measurements under similar conditions are more useful for identifying your personal baseline and changes over time.
