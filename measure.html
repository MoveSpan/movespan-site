Step 2 of 3 · Breathing Pattern
Where does your breathing begin?
Place one hand on your upper chest and the other on your abdomen. Take several calm, natural breaths. Do not deliberately deepen or change your breathing. Notice where the movement begins and where it feels strongest.
① Place one hand below the collarbones on the upper chest.
② Place the other hand over the abdomen around the navel.
③ Observe 5–8 natural breaths before choosing an answer.
