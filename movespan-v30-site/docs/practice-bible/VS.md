## Description

Voice & Sound combines vocal resonance, sound vibration, conscious breathing and therapeutic listening to regulate the nervous system, improve body awareness and restore psycho-emotional balance.

Unlike Breathwork, the primary therapeutic mechanism is **vibration and resonance**, not respiration.
