Small, regular practice matters

Why take assessments?

Assessments help MoveSpan understand your current mobility, balance, strength, recovery, and functional age. Your results are used to adjust practice intensity, exercise selection, and progress tracking.

What you’ll get

  • A clearer picture of your current abilities
  • More accurate practice recommendations
  • Baseline results for tracking progress
  • Suggestions for areas that need attention
Time required: about 5–10 minutes
You can complete assessments one at a time.
