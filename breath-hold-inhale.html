Breath Hold — After Inhale

This measurement looks at how comfortably you can hold your breath after a normal deep inhale.

How to perform the test

Take 1–3 slow, deep breaths. Then inhale comfortably, hold your breath, and stop the timer when you need to breathe.

Do not hyperventilate before the test.

Stop immediately if you feel dizzy or uncomfortable.
