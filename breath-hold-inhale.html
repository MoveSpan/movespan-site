Breath Hold — Inhale

Measure how long you can comfortably hold your breath after a full inhale.

1
Sit comfortably and rest for about 1–2 minutes.
2
Breathe normally. Do not hyperventilate before the test.
3
Take 1–3 slow, deep breaths. After your final comfortable inhale, hold your breath and tap the circle to start.
4
When you feel the need to breathe, tap the circle again to stop and record your result. Do not strain for extra seconds.
Stay seated during the test. Stop immediately if you feel dizzy, faint, chest discomfort, or unusual distress.
