Breath Hold — Inhale

This test measures how long you can comfortably hold your breath after a full inhale.

1
Sit comfortably and rest for about 1–2 minutes.
2
Breathe normally. Do not hyperventilate before the test.
3
Take one full comfortable inhale, then tap Start and hold your breath.
4
Tap Stop when you need to breathe. Do not strain for extra seconds.
Stay seated during the test. Stop immediately if you feel dizzy, faint, chest discomfort, or unusual distress.
