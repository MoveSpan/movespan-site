Orthostatic Response

This test checks how your pulse changes from lying down to standing.

How to perform the test

Rest lying down for 5 minutes if needed, record your pulse, stand up safely, wait 60 seconds, then measure your pulse again.

If you feel dizzy or unsteady, stop the test and sit or lie down.
