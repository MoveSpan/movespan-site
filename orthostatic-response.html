Orthostatic Response

Measure how your pulse changes when you move from lying down to standing.

1
Lie down comfortably and remain still for 5 minutes so your pulse can settle.
2
Measure and save your resting pulse while you are still lying down.
3
Stand up safely, wait 60 seconds, then measure and save your pulse again.
If you feel dizzy, faint, or unsteady when standing, stop the test and sit or lie down.

How to find your pulse
1
Choose one side of your neck.
2
Place your index and middle fingers gently in the soft groove beside your windpipe, just below the jaw.
3
Move your fingers slightly until you clearly feel each heartbeat. Press lightly. Do not use your thumb.
