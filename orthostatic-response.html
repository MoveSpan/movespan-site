Orthostatic Response

This test checks how your pulse changes from lying down to standing. It helps improve the accuracy of your functional profile.

  1. 1
    If you have been lying down quietly for at least 5 minutes already, there is no need to wait another 5 minutes.
  2. 2
    Otherwise, lie down and rest for 5 minutes first.
  3. 3
    Then record your resting pulse while you are still lying down, stand up, wait 60 seconds, and measure your pulse again.
If you feel dizzy or unsteady, stop the test and sit or lie down again.
