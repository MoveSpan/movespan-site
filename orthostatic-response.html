Orthostatic Response

This test looks at how your heart rate responds when you move from lying down to standing. It is easiest to do when you are already resting or lying down.

1
Start when you have been lying down or resting quietly for several minutes. If you are already relaxed and resting, there is no need to wait another 5 minutes.
2
Measure your pulse while still lying down.
3
Stand up normally and safely.
4
After 60 seconds of standing, measure your pulse again.
You only need to record your resting pulse, stand up, wait 60 seconds, and measure your pulse again. Stand near a stable support. If you feel dizzy or unsteady, sit or lie down immediately and do not continue the test.
