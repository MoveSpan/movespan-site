Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how MoveSpan collects, uses and protects information when you use MoveSpan and connect health or fitness services such as WHOOP.

Last updated: August 7, 2026

1. Information we collect

MoveSpan may collect information that you provide directly, including profile information, movement goals, practice preferences, body areas, pain or discomfort information, assessments, practice feedback and other information you choose to share.

When you connect a supported health or fitness service, MoveSpan may also receive health and fitness information that you authorize that service to share.

2. WHOOP data

If you choose to connect your WHOOP account, MoveSpan may access information permitted by the scopes you approve during the WHOOP authorization process.

This may include:

  • recovery information;
  • heart rate variability (HRV);
  • resting heart rate;
  • sleep information;
  • cycle and strain information;
  • workout information;
  • respiratory information;
  • body measurements;
  • profile information;
  • other WHOOP data specifically authorized by you.

3. How MoveSpan uses health data

Health and fitness information may be used to personalize MoveSpan recommendations and help determine appropriate practice type, duration, intensity and recovery needs.

MoveSpan may combine connected-device information with your assessments, practice history, subjective feedback and current body state to improve future recommendations.

MoveSpan does not use a single wearable metric as the sole basis for a practice recommendation. Health signals are considered together with other information and MoveSpan safety rules.

4. Personalization and learning

MoveSpan is designed to learn from your interaction with the system over time.

Practice completion, exercise feedback, selected modifications, subjective state and connected health signals may be used to improve the relevance of future recommendations.

5. Storage and security

MoveSpan uses reasonable technical and organizational safeguards designed to protect personal information.

Connected-service credentials and authorization tokens should be stored securely and are not intended to be exposed publicly or embedded in client-side application code.

6. Sharing and sale of data

MoveSpan does not sell personal health information to advertisers or data brokers.

Information may be processed by service providers that are necessary to operate MoveSpan, such as authentication, hosting, database and infrastructure providers.

7. Your control

Connecting a health or fitness service is optional. You may choose not to connect a service and continue using available MoveSpan features that do not require that connection.

You may disconnect a connected service through MoveSpan when that functionality is available, or revoke access through the connected service itself.

8. Data deletion

You may request deletion of personal information associated with your MoveSpan account.

Where technically and legally applicable, MoveSpan will delete or anonymize information associated with the request, subject to information that must be retained for security, legal or operational reasons.

9. Health information disclaimer

MoveSpan provides movement, wellness and educational tools. It is not a medical diagnosis service and is not a substitute for professional medical advice, diagnosis or treatment.

Wearable-device measurements can contain errors or uncertainty and should not be treated as independent medical diagnoses.

10. Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as MoveSpan adds new functionality, connected services or data processing features.

The current version will be published on this page with the date of the latest update.

11. Contact

Questions or requests concerning privacy and personal information can be submitted through MoveSpan's official website and support channels.
