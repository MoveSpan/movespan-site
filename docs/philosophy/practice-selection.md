# MoveSpan Practice Selection Philosophy

**Status:** Approved  
**Date:** July 2026  

---

## Foundation First

Every MoveSpan program begins with one essential practice:

**Joint Recovery (JR)**

Joint Recovery is the foundation of the MoveSpan System and is automatically included in every personalized program. Users do not need to choose it — it is always there.

---

## Free Plan

Users receive:
- ✅ Joint Recovery (included by default)
- Choose up to **2 Focus Practices**
- One opportunity to change their selection

> *Start with a strong foundation. Focus on what matters most today.*

The goal is not to limit the user but to encourage consistency and build sustainable habits.

---

## Premium (Essential)

Users receive:
- ✅ Joint Recovery (always included)
- Unlimited access to every MoveSpan practice
- Switch practices anytime
- AI continuously adapts the program as the body and goals evolve

> *Your body changes. Your practice can change with it.*

## Product Philosophy

MoveSpan does not sell access to more exercises.  
**MoveSpan sells the freedom to evolve.**

The free experience provides a focused starting point with a strong foundation. Premium removes limitations and allows the practice to grow together with the user, while AI continuously personalizes the journey.

---

## Core UX Principle

Never communicate Premium as "more locked features."

Instead communicate:
> *As your body evolves, your MoveSpan practice evolves with it.*

Or shorter:
> *Choose your focus today. Change your path as you grow.*

---

## Connection to Program Modes

This philosophy aligns with Restore → Reset → Expand:

- **Restore** — foundation + one focus practice, deep not wide
- **Reset** — foundation + two focus practices, building consistency  
- **Expand** — unlimited practices, AI-driven personalization
