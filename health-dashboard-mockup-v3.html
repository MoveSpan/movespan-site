Why this matters
Efficient breathing supports the brain and body
When breathing becomes faster or deeper than the body currently needs, ventilation may exceed metabolic demand. This may reduce carbon dioxide levels. Carbon dioxide helps regulate cerebral blood flow and supports the release of oxygen from hemoglobin into tissues.
Brain circulation
CO₂ helps regulate the diameter of blood vessels supplying the brain.
Oxygen delivery
CO₂ participates in the release of oxygen from hemoglobin into active tissues.
Calm regulation
Quieter breathing can support relaxation and reduce unnecessary respiratory effort.
The MoveSpan goal is not to take the deepest possible breath. It is to develop quiet, comfortable belly breathing with freer diaphragm movement, softer exhalation and less unnecessary tension in the upper chest, neck and shoulders.
Breathing rate and movement pattern alone do not diagnose hyperventilation or low CO₂. MoveSpan uses them as wellness observations for personalizing practices.
