Resting pulse and breathing rate are two simple indicators of how calmly and efficiently your body is working.

For many people over 40–50, chronic stress, shallow breathing and lack of movement cause both to rise. Regular MoveSpan practice gradually changes this — as the nervous system becomes more resilient, resting pulse and breathing tend to decrease or stabilise.

When to measure: Morning right after waking is the cleanest reading. Evening shows how much tension accumulated during the day. The app accepts readings at any time — both are useful.

Don't focus on individual readings — what matters is the trend over several weeks. Compare yourself to yourself a month ago, not to other people.
